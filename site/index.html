kyfram is a keyframe-driven rendering engine. You write down what shapes look like at specific times in a script. Go interpolates between keyframes and renders each frame to video. Retiming is editing a number, not re-directing callbacks.

Install

curl -fsSL https://codetesla51.gitlab.io/kyfram/install.sh | sh

Needs Go 1.25+ and ffmpeg. No prebuilt binaries. The script clones and builds for your machine. Pin a version with sh -s -- v0.2.0.

Quickstart

examples/intro.lgv 
scene(2, 30)

shape("ball", "circle", table{
    "0": table{x: 100, y: 180, r: 40, color: "#e64c40"},
    "2": table{x: 540, y: 180, r: 40, color: "#e64c40"},
})

Times are quoted seconds. Colors lerp per channel. Past the last keyframe, values hold.

kyfram render examples/intro.lgv --out out.mp4

Quality rides along: --crf 18 --preset slow for near-lossless, --format png for one frame with no ffmpeg.

kyfram view examples/intro.lgv

Opens localhost:8080 with a scrub slider and hot reload. Save the file, the preview follows.

What it does

Declarative keyframes

States at times, quoted in seconds. Numbers and colors lerp, text snaps to the earlier key.

Easing per segment

Linear, ease in and out, cubic, sine. Declared on the earlier key, owned by the engine.

Rich primitives

Circle, rect, polygon, star, wave, path, text on a path, images. Gradients and blend modes included.

Groups and camera

Children orbit a local origin. Pan and zoom the whole scene with keyframed cameras.

Audio and voice

Mux tracks under the video. Neural text to speech returns word timings for synced captions.

Built for batch

Template vars and text files turn one script into many videos. Preview hot, render cold.
